# P06-09 TABLE-004/005 static HWPX profiles to exact-target v2

The two predecessor contracts remain the admission authority for their bounded transformation
semantics:

- `hwpx-agent.table-text-to-table-hwpx-contract/v1`
- `hwpx-agent.table-table-to-text-hwpx-contract/v1`

The target v2 contract adds evidence, not broader conversion semantics. It binds deterministic
fixtures to one identified Hangul 2020 executable and requires create-only save, close, reopen,
readback, PDF export, process cleanup, independent typed HWPX oracle, and visual render review.

The target run established that Hangul 2020 uses a run-direct native table wrapper and canonicalizes
redundant namespace declarations on generated paragraph fragments. The product now emits and admits
that reviewed shape. The prior single-control TABLE-005 wrapper remains a separately admitted exact
legacy profile; arbitrary, mixed, or ambiguous native wrappers are not admitted.

Existing documents are not rewritten in place. Failed and superseded attempts remain immutable and
may not be promoted. Target v2 does not enable automation routing, establish differential equality,
or claim arbitrary user documents, another PC/version, P06 completion, production, or product
completion.
