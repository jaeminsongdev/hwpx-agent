# P08 object conformance v0 to v1

- `v0` had separate component reports and no authoritative 58-row target matrix.
- `v1` binds every `OBJ-001` through `OBJ-058` row to the generated capability contract,
  direct evidence stage, target-binding state, fixture/validator requirements and blockers.
- Asset preservation is exact by path, relationship, media type, expanded length and SHA-256.
- Render comparison has zero structural, missing-glyph and unchanged-region tolerance. Attempt 18
  binds narrow affected-region limits to seven exact fixtures and passes the other 34 direct cases
  pixel-exact at 96 DPI; this target calibration does not transfer to other fixtures or builds.
- Component and synthetic evidence can advance only to their recorded stage. It cannot satisfy
  target-build, installed-Hangul, render, automation, differential or release-green fields.
- The final P08 projection is phase-aware: 41 supported P08 rows are green, `OBJ-044` remains
  non-green as exact-target unavailable, and all 16 P11-owned rows remain non-green/deferred.
- Desktop automation is a fallback for P08. A verified fallback requires exact-target runtime and
  same-contract semantic differential evidence. An unavailable fallback is accepted only with an
  exact reason, disabled routing, and an independently oracle-verified preferred direct route.
- Existing private documents and failed evidence are referenced read-only and are never migrated
  in place.
